Standard Operating Procedure: First-Time Master Data Onboarding

Follow this strictly ordered checklist when onboarding a live business onto AgriBOS ERP.

Stage 1 — Financial Accounts & Opening Cash

Register the business cash box, bank current/savings accounts, and UPI wallets. Set the accurate opening cash and bank balances as of the cutoff date. These balances initialize the ledger without creating phantom revenue.

Stage 2 — Fleet Machinery & Baseline Meter Readings

Register every tractor, harvester, JCB, and implement with its unique registration number and current hour/kilometer meter reading. Meter readings establish fuel efficiency baselines.

Stage 3 — Farmers / Regular Customers

Onboard regular agricultural clients with village locations and contact numbers. If clients carry opening due balances from prior seasons, enter them in the opening balance field.

Stage 4 — Fuel & Spare Parts Suppliers

Register diesel petrol pumps, spare parts distributors, and mechanical workshops. Record any outstanding credit opening dues for accurate liability tracking.

Stage 5 — Staff, Drivers & Compensation Wage Rules

Add drivers, harvester operators, and mechanics. Configure their authoritative wage rates (e.g. daily wage allowance, monthly salary, or per-acre harvesting commission).

Stage 6 — Opening Balance Reconciliation & Verification

Navigate to the Opening Balance Reconciliation screen. Ensure that total opening funds + customer receivables - supplier payables equals the verified initial capital of the business.
