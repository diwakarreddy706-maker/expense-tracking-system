Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: {% now "F d, Y" %} | Sri Basaveshwara & Co. Enterprise Operations

1. Purpose & Scope of Operations

This Privacy Policy governs the processing, retention, and access protocols for operational and financial data recorded within AgriBOS ERP, operated by Sri Basaveshwara & Co. This platform is a private, role-gated business ledger used exclusively by authorized management, accountants, operators, and field supervisors to record machinery telemetry, diesel dispensing, staff wages, and agricultural harvesting contracts.

2. Information Recorded

The system stores operational data required for farm contracting and business auditing:

  • Farmer / Client Information: Name, contact phone number, billing address/village, land location, and credit ledger balance (Udhar Katha).
  • Fleet & Equipment Telemetry: Machine serials, hour meter readings, GPS acreage, diesel consumption slips, and breakdown service logs.
  • Staff & Crew Records: Driver names, phone numbers, assigned machinery, attendance, advance draws, and wage payout disbursements.
  • Audit Logging: Timestamp, operator username, IP address, and mutation changes for all financial and master record modifications.

3. Cookie & Client Storage Standards

AgriBOS ERP employs strictly necessary first-party cookies solely to maintain authenticated user sessions and prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). The application contains zero third-party advertising trackers, zero commercial analytics beacons, and zero cross-site behavioral telemetry.

4. Data Security & Access Controls

Access to financial ledgers, bank transactions, and employee compensation is strictly restricted via Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS cryptographic ciphers, and automated disaster-recovery snapshots are archived daily with AES encryption.

5. Contact & Data Administrator

For inquiries regarding record rectifications, ledger audits, or data deletion requests, contact the System Administrator at Sri Basaveshwara & Co. headquarters.
